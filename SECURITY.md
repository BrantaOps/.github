# Security policy

Report a security issue to [support@branta.pro](mailto:support@branta.pro). We will review it and respond as quickly as we can.

The same policy is published at [branta.pro/disclosure](https://branta.pro/disclosure).

## Scope

- https://branta.pro
- https://guardrail.branta.pro
- Public repositories in the [BrantaOps](https://github.com/BrantaOps) organization

## Out of scope

- UI and UX issues with no security impact
- Denial of service
- Social engineering of Branta staff or customers
- Services operated by someone other than Branta

## What to send

- A description of the issue
- The steps to reproduce it
- The URL or repository where it appears

## Before you publish

Give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before you publish it. Do not access, change, or destroy customer data beyond what is needed to show the issue. Do not interrupt the service.

This policy covers security issues only.
